## Three decisive moments
1. **Line 5→6:** "זה נשמע די רגיל" ("this sounds pretty ordinary") → "בואו נחשוב על כיוון אחר לגמרי. מה הופך את ההחלקות שאתם עושים למיוחדות…?" ("let's think of a completely different direction. What makes your straightening special…?"). The promise of a new idea turned into a questionnaire. From here on there was no creative value in the conversation.
2. **Line 12→14:** "פנו... רק שאלו על מחיר, ולא קבעו תור" ("people contacted me... only asked about price, and didn't book") → "לא מספיק חידדה את הערך... מה ההחלקות שלכם נותנות…?" ("didn't sharpen the value enough... what does your straightening give…?"). The key insight was received, and it got an answer that is generic marketing language plus another question. That triggered "גם הקודם אמר שיהיה איכותי" ("the previous one also said it would be high quality").
3. **Line 15→16:** "אני צריכה לחשוב על זה" ("I need to think about it") → a long paragraph of terms + "איך זה נשמע?" ("how does that sound?"). A closing attempt after a request to think, plus an offer of a summary document that has not been approved yet.

## Caveat on the conclusion
The answers were produced by **gemini-2.5-flash**, not by claude-sonnet-5, which Meital runs on in real conversations. So:
- **Conditions independent of the model are checked:** the prompt allows these failures. There is no rule that requires a new idea after "רגיל" ("ordinary"), and no rule that ties the past disappointment to the idea.
- **The quality of the wording and the humour are not proven** for the real model.

## Proposed targeted fix (not applied; waiting for approval)
Three lines in the stage-1 prompt, sections on creativity and objections:
1. **After "רגיל/גנרי/לא מצחיק" ("ordinary/generic/not funny"):** the next reply **must contain a new idea that is different in kind**. A question is allowed only after the idea, never instead of it.
2. **When the customer says past advertising brought price inquiries without appointments:**
   - say briefly that this is the gap a good ad closes: giving a reason to book, not to ask the price;
   - give an idea built around that, such as a punchline that makes viewers book rather than ask;
   - say once that the ad is the product, and that distribution, targeting and budget are not included.
3. **After "צריכה לחשוב" ("I need to think"):**
   - at most one short question about what is missing, or a goodbye;
   - **no recital of terms and no "איך זה נשמע?"** ("how does that sound?");
   - **do not offer a summary** while stage 2 is not enabled.
